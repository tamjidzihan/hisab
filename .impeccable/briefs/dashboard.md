# Surface Brief: Dashboard

<!-- impeccable:brief-schema 1 -->

## Scope & Mode

- Target: `dashboard`
- Mode: Operate
- Audience: Individual personal finance tracker managing monthly budget and multi-account balances (BDT).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Personal financial management structured with drafting-table precision rather than generic SaaS cards—every taka budgeted, transferred, or saved with dimensional accountability. Refuses the generic purple-accented white card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool drafting-sheet ground (#f4f6f9), technical graphite ink (#161d26), structural border rules (#d2d9e4), and amber caliper markings (#d97706) for budget thresholds, paired with strict tabular monospaced numbers.

STORY: The user immediately sees net cash flow, verifies account balances across cash and mobile wallets, spots approaching budget thresholds on dimensioned calipers, and enters new transactions with zero friction.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Three-column layout on desktop: left rail with account vaults, center column with month cash-flow metrics (income, expenses, net) and high-density transaction ledger, right rail with dimension-caliper budget bars. Primary Quick Add transaction action pinned in center-top.
